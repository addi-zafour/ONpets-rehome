How Rehoming Works

  1. Apply: Click WhatsApp button for the dog you love
  2. Chat: We ask about your home, yard, experience
  3. Home Check: We visit or ask for video of your yard (Rusape area)
  4. Adoption Fee: Pay via Ecocash / Innbucks
  5. Follow-up: We check after 1 week - how is your new dog?

We don't give dogs to just anyone. We match each dog to the RIGHT family.
